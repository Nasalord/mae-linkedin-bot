# Vivian Liu: LinkedIn voice and style guide

**Source:** `vivian-posts.csv` (same folder), collected 2 Nov 2026 from Vivian's personal activity feed (Posts filter) and the Mae - Making Auto Easy company page. Read only.

**What the analysis covers:** 34 posts that Vivian wrote herself during the Mae era (Mar 2022 to Oct 2026). These are her original posts plus her own text on reposts, with at least 20 words. Pure reposts, posts from her GM/Autozen years, and one-line reposts are in the CSV but left out of the statistics, because they don't show her Mae voice.

**About the numbers:** Reactions, comments and reposts are the counts as of 2 Nov 2026. Dates come from LinkedIn post IDs (UTC). The format column was detected automatically from the page, so treat it as approximate. Every quote below is copied word for word.

## 1. Voice in three words

**Candid, celebratory, scrappy.**

- **Candid:** she admits fear and mistakes ("I had one fear: What if no one shows up to my session?"; "We threw out our AI product and started over.").
- **Celebratory:** wins are shared loudly, with ALL CAPS, stretched words and gratitude ("IT'S OFFICIAL!", "Ahhh!!", "Annnnd...").
- **Scrappy:** she writes as the underdog founder in a suits industry ("dressed like a SF tech bro", "The new kids on the block", "the VERY obvious founder in the room").

- **Line breaks:** short paragraphs of one to three sentences. She often stacks single-line fragments for rhythm ("Building in auto isn't easy. / It's relationship-driven. / Trust-based. / And earned.").
- **Lists:** numbered lists (1., 1️⃣) or dashes for criteria, highlights and tips. Bullets (•) appear only once.
- **Emojis:** most used are 💛 (13), 🚀 (8), ✨ (8), 🙌 (4) and 💸 (4). They go at the end of a line, never in the middle of a sentence. Doubled emojis are common (✨💛, 🥳🥳, 🤯🤯, 💛💛). Her 2026 insight posts use very few (0 to 4).
- **Hashtags:** always at the very end, on their own line, 3 to 6 typically. Her most used tags are #womeninautomotive (9), #bcbusiness (8), #womeninauto (5), #startup (5), #autostartup (4) and #femalefounder(s) (8). Since 2025 she uses more #autotech, #autostartup, #dealershipAI and #automotivetech.
- **Tagging:** she tags the company page "Mae - Making Auto Easy" in 23 of 34 posts, and names and thanks people and organizations inline. Long name lists usually come near the end, for example the ASOTU post tags about 20 people.

### Opening patterns

- **First-person past-tense confession plus a twist:** "I fired…", "I walked into…", "I found… by accident", "We threw out…". The hook makes a claim that sounds risky or contrarian, and the post explains it.
- **"First" framing:** first REAL talk, first customer, first year, first NADA booth, first article.
- **A concrete number or rank in line 1:** 250 investors, $700,000, top 3, 8 year anniversary, 6 gruelling weeks.
- **Short sentence plus a fragment:** "…started over. Best decision ever."
- **Loud announcement:** ALL CAPS or stretched letters ("IT'S OFFICIAL!", "BEYOND", "Ahhh!!", "Annnnd...").
- 11 of 34 posts start with "I" or "My". The opening line is rarely a question. The only true question opener is a low performer ("What does it take to get into and through Techstars?", 26 reactions). Two more open by quoting a question someone asked: "What do you hate?" and "Do you have any basic tips…".

## 4. Endings and CTAs

Most posts end in one of five ways, often combined:

1. **A short punchline line or kicker:** "That's not a training problem. That's a memory problem." / "Maybe that's exactly where I belong." / "Switching gears in 2026, LFG. 🚀" / "Work, play, and way too much sugar."
2. **Gratitude with named people:** "Thank you to Veronica Dunford and Joe Webb from Women In Automotive for pushing me to speak when I didn't think I had anything to say."
3. **A soft invitation, not a hard CTA:** "If you're in SF (especially in tech and auto), let's connect! 💛🚗" / "If we met you, we're very grateful. / If we haven't, we'd love to." / "If you or someone you know needs help buying a car, DM me, I gotchu!"
4. **A lone emoji sign-off:** "💛" on its own line, or a closing emoji pair.
5. **A hashtag block** as the very last line.

She almost never ends with an engagement-bait question: the median post has zero question marks. Asks are explicit and specific when they appear, for example "4 more days to vote…", "I'm looking for 20 women…", "Full podcast in the comments", or "'Mae Sales Assistant' is live now on iOS and Android! 📱".

## 9. Company page vs. Vivian

- **The company page is effectively dormant.** Only one post is visible on the Mae company page, both on the Posts tab and in a content search filtered to the company. It is the NADA booth recap (Feb 2026: 25 reactions, 0 comments, 1 repost).
- **Voice:** that post is written in "we" voice. It uses a numbered list plus dash bullets, has no emojis, ends with four hashtags plus a tag for NADA, and is warm but plain ("The new kids on the block.", "If we met you, we're very grateful.").
- **Reach:** Vivian's quote-repost of the same post, with her own 117 words on top, got 76 reactions and 16 comments, about three times the company post.
- **Takeaway:** all the reach sits on Vivian's personal profile. One post is too few to define a separate company voice. If the company page is revived, write it as the same voice in "we", with fewer emojis.

## 11. Rules for an AI drafting in Vivian's voice

1. **Open with a one-line hook in first person** that states a concrete event, decision or number ("I fired my first customer and I'd do it again."). No question openers.
2. **Put a specific number in the hook or the second line** (dollars, percentages, counts, rank, years). Her best posts all have one.
3. **Use "first" framing when it's true:** first talk, first booth, first customer.
4. **Pair every win with one honest admission** of fear, doubt or a mistake.
5. **Keep it between 100 and 220 words.** Go longer only for event recaps with many people to thank.
6. **Write one to three sentences per paragraph,** with a blank line between paragraphs. Use stacked one-line fragments for rhythm at the turn or the ending.
7. **Use numbered lists or dashes** for criteria, lessons or highlights. Keep each item to one line.
8. **Add emphasis with ALL CAPS on one or two words per post** (REAL, BEYOND, VERY, MORE). Use a stretched word ("sooo", "Annnnd") at most once.
9. **Use 0 to 4 emojis**, at the ends of lines, never mid-sentence. Prefer 💛 🚀 ✨ 🙌. Use fewer emojis on product or insight posts.
10. **Tag "Mae - Making Auto Easy" once,** at the first mention. After that, write "Mae".
11. **Describe Mae as helping humans, not replacing them.** Show its value through a customer moment or a metric, not a feature list.
12. **Use real dealership language** (GM, showroom floor, service drive, CRM, CSI, turnover), the way an insider would.
13. **Use casual words sparingly.** One per post at most, for example LFG, "So. Damn. Cool.", or a car pun. Profanity only when quoting herself.
14. **Thank named people** for specific things, near the end.
15. **End with a kicker line, an invitation** ("If you're in…, let's connect!"), **or 💛**. Don't end with "What do you think?" or similar engagement-bait questions.
16. **Make CTAs concrete and rare:** vote by a date, DM me, link in comments, app is live.
17. **Put 2 to 6 hashtags on the final line only.** Current set: #autotech #autostartup #dealershipAI #automotivetech #womeninautomotive.
18. **Avoid corporate filler:** leverage, synergy, delve, thought leader, "excited to announce", "game-changing" (except about people).
19. **Avoid generic how-to listicles without a personal story.** They are her lowest performers.
20. **For milestones, post with your own photos** (multi-image does best). Use reposts only when you add your own story on top.
21. **Never invent metrics, customers or quotes.** Every number she uses is specific and checkable.
