## Draft (132 words)

```
Dealers spent more on advertising last quarter and sold fewer cars.

A benchmark of 4,000+ franchised stores put ad cost at about $411 per vehicle sold, up 6.3%. Volume went down.

The usual answer is to buy more traffic. The better question is where the traffic you already paid for goes.

After consulting 80+ GM dealerships, these are the three leaks I'd check first:

1. The conversation. The shopper tells the salesperson their budget, trade and timeline. Almost none of it gets written down.

2. The follow-up. They don't buy today. Whoever calls back doesn't know what was said.

3. The exit. The salesperson leaves, and every conversation in their head walks out with them.

Your next $411 won't fix any of those.

Which of the three costs your store the most?
```
